### Hello! (⊃｡•́‿•̀｡)⊃

This year, I've worked freelance as an **AI & NLP mentor**, for [Wild Code School](https://www.wildcodeschool.com/), and [Centrale Supélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/), helping professionals and engineering students boost their skills and knowledge.
